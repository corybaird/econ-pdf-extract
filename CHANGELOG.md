# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-24

Initial public release (beta)

- Claim, statistic and formula extraction from economics PDFs with page and section provenance
- `econ-pdf` CLI: `extract`, `report`, `targets`, `eval`
- Optional spaCy (`[nlp]`) and GLM-OCR backends
- Public benchmark in [docs/benchmark.md](docs/benchmark.md)
