# Citations for the public benchmark papers

The four papers in the public benchmark ([`docs/benchmark.md`](benchmark.md)), with the URLs whose SHA-256 checksums are recorded in [`references/benchmarks/papers.yaml`](../references/benchmarks/papers.yaml).

- Marco Jacopo Lombardi, Taejin Park and Fernando Perez-Cruz (2026), "MaRCo@BIS: AI-agentic econometric analysis for central bankers", BIS Working Paper No 1395, Monetary and Economic Department, Bank for International Settlements, November 2026. https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1395-marcobis-ai-agentic-econometric-analysis-central-bankers.pdf
- Luiz Brandao-Marques, Giovanni Casiraghi, Gaston Gelos, Ryan Harrison and Gunes Kamber (2023), "Is high debt constraining monetary policy? Evidence from inflation expectations", BIS Working Paper No 1141, Monetary and Economic Department, Bank for International Settlements. https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1141-high-debt-constraining-monetary-policy-evidence-inflation-expectations.pdf
- Andres Blanco, Pablo Ottonello and Tereza Ranosova (2022), "The Dynamics of Large Inflation Surges", NBER Working Paper No 30555, National Bureau of Economic Research. https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w30555/w30555.pdf
- Eric T. Swanson (2023), "The Macroeconomic Effects of the Federal Reserve's Conventional and Unconventional Monetary Policies", NBER Working Paper No 31603, National Bureau of Economic Research. https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w31603/w31603.pdf

Two additional working papers are bundled with the repo as committed sample PDFs under `examples/papers/` and exercise the same `econ-pdf` pipeline:

- Miguel Acosta, Andrea Ajello, Michael Bauer, Francesca Loria and Silvia Miranda-Agripino (2026), "Financial Market Effects of FOMC Communication: Evidence from a New Event-Study Database", Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco Working Paper 2025-30 (bundled as `examples/papers/acosta_et_al_2025-30_usmpd.pdf`). https://doi.org/10.24148/wp2025-30
- Adam Hale Shapiro, Moritz Sudhof and Daniel Wilson (2020), "Measuring News Sentiment", Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco Working Paper 2017-01 (bundled as `examples/papers/shapiro_2017-01_sentiment.pdf`). https://doi.org/10.24148/wp2017-01 and http://www.frbsf.org/economic-research/publications/working-papers/2017/01/

These papers are redistribution-restricted: download the PDFs yourself, do not commit them (see [`docs/benchmark.md`](benchmark.md)).
